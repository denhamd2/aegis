### What was taken, and what was left

**Superseded.** This section used to record that only Color and Roughness
were imported, at 512px with mipmaps off, because no admissible visual
capture existed and normal maps could not be shown to buy anything.
`ARCHITECTURE.md`'s amended renderer rule retired that premise.

The current, authoritative record of what is imported and why lives beside
the files, in `materials/CREDITS.md`. In short: 1K PNG, Color + NormalGL +
Roughness + AmbientOcclusion (+ Metalness where shipped), mipmaps on, no
displacement. `core/materials/material_library.gd` resolves all of it.

The albedo textures are still used as *variance* on top of a value the tint
carries, so `tools/refs/measure_frame.py`'s luminance relationships survive.
The one exception is documented there as `preserve_albedo_mean`.

### IP guardrail

Per `gauntlet/anchor/ARCHITECTURE.md`: these are generic CC0 surface scans.
No WWE-, AEW- or any other promotion-derived geometry, textures, logos, trade
dress or likenesses are used anywhere in this directory, and none may be
added. The arena geometry itself is generated by
`game/tools/arena/build_arena.gd` from primitives — it is not a third-party
arena model, and deliberately so:

- Branded arena models that exist publicly are either extracted game assets or
  replicas of trademarked trade dress. Both are out under the IP guardrail.
- The ring's own dimensions are load-bearing for measurement: `camera.md`
  derives the 41° FOV from the ropes sitting at 3.1 m, `match_camera.gd` picks
  its 1.45 m eye height by unprojecting those rope heights, and
  `test_camera_framing.gd` and `grapple_rig.gd` pin constants to the 6 m mat.
  Third-party ring geometry would invalidate that chain.
