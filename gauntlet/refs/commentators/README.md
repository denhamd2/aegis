# Commentary character references

The two sheets in this directory were generated from the attached AEW portrait
references to guide the game's seated commentary models, material palette and
small broadcast gestures. They are production references; the game uses the
separate modeled GLB assets.

## Tony Schiavone

`tony_schiavone_sheet.png` emphasizes his current recognizable traits: short
side-parted brown/gray hair, blue rectangular glasses, full dark beard, broad
build and burgundy blazer with a patterned tie. Source references:

- [AEW's Tony Schiavone broadcast roster profile](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/broadcast-roster/tony-schiavone), including the current official portrait and role.
- [Recent 2026 AEW Revolution interview photo](https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2129126/aew-revolution-2026-tony-schiavone-hangman-adam-page-blood-lens/).

## Excalibur

`excalibur_sheet.png` keeps the defining black, white and metallic-gold mask,
its exposed mouth and beard, dark suit, white shirt, charcoal tie and headset.
Sources:

- [AEW's Excalibur interview, Part 1](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/getting-to-know-aew-excalibur-part-1), where he explains the mask's Great Sasuke inspiration.
- [AEW's Excalibur interview, Part 2](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/who-is-that-masked-man-getting-to-know-aew-s-excalibur-part-2), with an official suited character portrait.
- [Recent 2026 Dynamite coverage](https://www.pwtorch.com/site/2026/10/06/aew-dynamite-results-10-6-powells-review-of-will-ospreay-vs-kazuchika-okada-for-the-aew-world-championship-darby-allin-vs-kyle-fletcher-for-the-tnt-title/), confirming Excalibur and Schiavone on the current broadcast desk.

The source looks were checked on 15 October 2026. The Blender build script is
`tools/blender/commentators.py`; the generated game models are
`game/assets/environment/commentator_tony.glb` and
`game/assets/environment/commentator_excalibur.glb`.
