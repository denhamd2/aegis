# AEW crowd-lighting references — 7 November 2026

Owner-supplied stills for Release 1 of the
[incremental quality plan](../incremental_quality_plan.md).
Reference images remain reference material; do not embed them into game assets.

| Original | Reference | What to review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `71412.jpg` | [Wide arena view](71412.jpg) | The ring is the brightest large surface. Front rows remain readable; seating grows darker with distance and height. Upper stands and roof remain shadowed despite visible fixtures and ribbons. |
| `71411.jpg` | [Low ring view](71411.jpg) | Wrestler and ropes stay readable against a dark audience. Ribbon highlights do not lift the whole bowl. Avoid bright eyes or self-lit crowd faces. |
| `71410.jpg` | [Broadcast match view](71410.jpg) | Faces and shirts in nearby rows are distinguishable, with progressively darker seating behind. Ring exposure stays independent of audience fill. |

Tune existing crowd emission, depth response and audience spill rather than
raising global exposure or darkening the ring. Review the normal match camera
and low/entrance coverage; keep material and lighting settings consistent.
The photographs provide visual direction, not calibrated lux values or an
M1 performance measurement.

For release acceptance, record the before/after capture paths, actual renderer,
adapter and software-rasterisation status. Use Forward+ for visual judgement.
Run the repository's evidence gate for capture claims and leave native M1
frame pacing unverified until measured on the target hardware.
