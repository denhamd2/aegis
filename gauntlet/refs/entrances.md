# entrances.md — Roman Reigns and Cody Rhodes, and the plan to build them

Research for the per-wrestler ring entrances, and the build plan that follows
from it. Written the way the other refs are: every claim says where it came
from, and nothing here is presented as measured unless it was.

## How much of this is sourced

Entrance choreography lives in video, not text. The written record is thin,
and several of the best sources (the move wikis, the long-form breakdowns)
were paywalled or blocked from here. Each point below is tagged:

- **[S]** — stated in a cited source (list at the end).
- **[K]** — general, widely broadcast knowledge of these entrances or of
  wrestling TV production, NOT found in a source here. Treat as a strong
  prior, and confirm it on footage before it is tuned.
- **[P]** — a project decision for the game: a choice, not a fact.

Timings are **[P]** throughout. None has been frame-stepped. The repo's own
method for that exists (`camera.md`, `timings.md` were measured off supplied
footage). The single most valuable input for this round is one clean
broadcast entrance per man, supplied by the owner, to measure beats off.

One framing fact matters for authenticity: both men's defining entrances are
from **WWE** broadcasts [S: Roman's Tribal Chief era 2020–2024; Cody's
"Kingdom" since 2016, used in AEW until 2022 and in WWE since]. Cody's
entrance ran weekly on AEW Dynamite [S]; Roman's never has. The plan puts
each man's own choreography onto *this game's* AEW set and production.

### Roman [V]

**The wait is the entrance.** R-41: 0-18 s the stage is EMPTY -- a wide
establishing shot of the set under blue/cyan lasers, then a slow head-on
push into the tunnel mouth. He is first seen at **20 s**, deep in the
tunnel, walking straight at a static head-on long lens; he reaches the stage
front at **~30 s**. The music's quiet-then-slam (owner's track: near silence
42.5-44.9 s, the slam at **45.0 s**) is what the finger waits for.

**Stage.** R-41 30-40 s: stops at the front of the stage, full figure,
head-on; stands square, looks slowly left and right. **40 s: the finger** --
the RIGHT arm straight up, index finger to the sky, the rest of the fist
closed, chin level. **42 s: pyro** on the cut -- red gerbs both sides of the
stage and the light flips from blue to red/magenta for the burst -- and a
cut to a **very wide** of the whole stage. The finger is HELD, still, for
**~18 s** (40-58 s) under a low wide shot from the ramp, his name on the set
above him. R-CJ: the same finger, right arm, taken with Jey beside him.
R-XL (title era): the title goes up one-handed, right arm straight, plates
to camera, **eyes closed, head bowed** (1:00-1:04), then the pyro burst turns
the set from gold to blue.

**Face.** R-41 1:04-1:06: a tight telephoto close-up, head and shoulders,
eyes narrowed, before he moves.

**The walk.** R-41 1:08 to 2:40 -- **~90 s from stage to ringside**. The
signature shot is an **extreme low-angle ultra-wide steadicam backing
down the ramp ahead of him**: lens at hip height or lower, tilted up, him
chest-up against the roof and the lasers, head and ula fala at the top of
frame (1:18-1:52, 2:00-2:18). Intercut every ~6-8 s with **very wide
arena shots from high** (1:14, 1:26, 1:36, 1:44, 1:54, 2:20-2:40). Gait:
slow, square, arms hanging a hand's width off his sides with the palms
back, shoulders back, chin a little down, eyes up and out; he looks slowly
round the building, never at the camera, never breaks stride. R-XL walk
(1:34-2:06): the same, taken instead head-on at eye level, chest-up,
Heyman holding the title up behind him. R-CJ adds an **over-the-shoulder
steadicam behind him** looking down the ramp at the crowd (20-22 s,
46-52 s).

**Ringside.** R-41 2:42-3:04: stops at ringside, close eye-level
handheld: **head bowed, eyes closed** -- a held, private beat -- before the
steps. Then (3:06-3:08) a tight shot of his hand on the post, and up the
steps and in.

**Ring.** R-41 3:24-3:26: in the ring, arm and finger up again to the
crowd (hard cam, wide). R-39: with the titles, **arms out level to the
sides** holding one in each hand (14 s), then **both overhead** (16-28 s),
low camera from the apron up at him. Then hands on hips, staring
(R-41 3:42-3:54, tight).

**Light.** OTC and WM41: **blue/cyan** set and house, lasers; red only in
the pyro burst. The gold look belongs to the title-era set (R-XL's
orchestra), and even there it flips to blue after the pyro.

### Cody [V]

**Open.** C-SNME 0-8 s and C-MITB 4-8 s: black, then the silver
American-Nightmare skull wall video; wide shots of a dark, blue-lit house
and the crowd while the WHOA plays -- **he is not on camera for the first
WHOA**.

**Emerge.** C-MITB 22-26 s and C-SNME 20-24 s: out of **thick stage
smoke**, backlit in the portal, walking straight at a head-on long lens.

**The WHOA pose.** C-39 20-25 s: at the top of the stage, **arms straight
out level to the sides, palms forward, chest up, head back**, held; taken
head-on down the ramp on a long lens. **The pyro** (C-39 24 s): an air
burst of fireworks over the set, on a very wide. Then (C-39 26 s, and
8 s of the WHOA sheet) **both fists pumped down hard** -- the "punch" --
and confetti.

**Walk.** C-39 8-15 s (WHOA sheet): an **extreme low-angle wide steadicam
backing ahead of him**, the same grammar as Roman's, but he is moving fast
and looking about, mouth open, calling to the crowd. Profile close-ups
yelling (C-39 48-50 s). Gait: brisk, bouncy, shoulders rolling, arms
swinging, head turning side to side.

**The kneel.** C-MITB 40-44 s: at the top of the ramp he **drops to one
knee** -- right knee down, left foot planted, left forearm across the left
knee, head down -- holds, then looks up and stands. Long lens head-on up
the ramp. (C-SS 4 s: the same kneel.)

**The barricade.** C-SS 14-30 s, C-SNME 36-1:40: he walks down the aisle
**slapping hands along both barricades**, stopping to shout with fans;
handheld at barricade height alongside him. C-SNME 40-48 s: a **low wide
stance mid-aisle, knees bent, arms wide** -- the WHOA again, for the fans.
C-SNME 1:44: **opens the jacket with both hands** to show the title.

**Ring.** C-SNME 1:50-1:58: round the ringside mat to the steps, up them in
two strides, onto the apron at the corner, and **in over the ropes by the
post**; the camera is low **inside the ring behind him**, the coat's long
tail filling the frame as he walks to centre and turns to the hard cam.
None of these clips shows the middle-rope pose the sources describe; it
stays [S], unmeasured.

**Coat** (C-39, C-SNME): long, to mid-calf, fitted through the chest and
waist then **flaring from the hips**; a **stand collar, open to the waist**;
**gold fringed epaulettes**; C-39's is white and gold, C-SNME's red with
blue sleeves and white stripes. It swings wide when he turns.

**Light.** Blue house, red/white/blue stage and wall, white beams, heavy
fog at the portal.

## Roman Reigns — the Tribal Chief

**Character of the entrance.** Slow, deliberate, still. "Walks with a slow,
deliberate pace with his eyes fixed forward… no wasted movement" [S]. The
point of it is that he makes the building wait [S]. He does not play to the
crowd on the way down [K].

**Music.** "Head of the Table", debuted 10 May 2021, replacing the Shield-era
theme [S]. Deep bass and tribal drums [S]. *No audio ships in this game*; the
beats below are cut to where the music's hits would fall [P].

**Props and attire.**
- **The ula fala** — a Samoan chief's necklace of dried pandanus fruit,
  red/orange segments on a cord [S]; presented to him in 2020 after Hell in a
  Cell, by his father Sika and uncle Afa [S]. Worn for the entrance and
  removed in the ring [K].
- **The title** — carried over the shoulder; "the belts became an important
  part of his overall presentation" [S]. In this game he is AEW CHAMPION
  [P, owner's reference], so one belt, the AEW World Championship.
- Ring gear under it: black [K]; the game model already carries his own.
- Earlier eras came through the crowd in a tactical vest — the Shield
  look, used solo until March 2016 [S]. Not this entrance.
- Heyman often walks ahead of him [S], and at WM41 he entered alone [S].
  There is no Heyman model, so **he walks alone** [P].

**Signature moment.** The **raised index finger** — "raises the ☝️ as the
pyro goes off" [S]. He also raises the title as pyro fires [S]. He does it
at the top of the ramp and again in the ring [K].

## Cody Rhodes — the American Nightmare

**Character of the entrance.** Big, loud, participatory. The crowd sings the
song; the "WHOA" is theirs as much as his [S].

**Music.** "Kingdom" by Downstait, his theme since 2016 across TNA, NJPW,
ROH, AEW and WWE [S]. The opening "WHOA" nods to his earlier "Smoke and
Mirrors" [S].

**Props and attire.**
- An entrance jacket, snapped at the front: a replica is sold as
  "Deluxe Replica Entrance Full-Snap Jacket" [S], with glitter detailing on
  his entrance gear [S]. Taken off in the ring [K].
- In this game he holds no title [P], so no belt, and his lower third
  subtitle is his nickname.
- In AEW his wife Brandi and their dog Pharaoh sometimes came out with him —
  mortar pyro once sent the dog back up the tunnel [S]. Not in the game [P].

**Signature moments.**
- On the "WHOA", **he opens his arms and the crowd yells it with him** [S].
- **Pyro fires, and more of it when he punches the air** [S]. He gets more
  pyro than anyone in WWE, "record-setting" at WrestleMania 39 [S]; it lights
  "either side of the ramp" [S].
- He **poses on the stage, then climbs to the middle rope to look out into
  the crowd** [S].
- On the way down he punches the air and raises his fists [S].

**How WWE stages it** [S]/[K]. The building goes dark before the music.
"Kingdom" opens with the WHOA -- the crowd shouts it with the track -- and
Cody is found on the stage **in the dark**, arms flung wide; he "hits his
standard WHOA pose and waits for his pyro" [S], and when the full track
lands the lights slam up with the pyro, and "more ensues when he punches
the air" [S]. The house wiring is literal: WWE's pyro desk has a button
labelled *Cody* [S]. Then a slow, crowd-singing walk, and in the ring he
"climbs to the middle rope to look out into the crowd" [S].

## What a production director would add (both)

- **The lower third follows the man, not the clock** [K/P]. It keys on once
  he has hit his mark and the shot is settled, holds long enough to read
  (4–6 s), and keys off before the walk, so it never covers him moving. Roman's
  comes off on the finger so nothing competes with the pyro; Cody's comes on
  after the pyro, because nothing should cover the WHOA.
- **Coverage** [K]: a wide establishing shot of the stage; a long lens
  head-on from the ramp foot; a **steadicam** backing down the ramp in front
  of him; a hard cam; a low ringside handheld for the steps; a low in-ring
  angle up at him for the corner pose; crowd cutaways. Cut on the music's
  hits.
- **Pace sells character**: Roman ~1.0 m/s and never breaks stride; Cody
  ~1.6 m/s and stops to work the crowd [S: "slow walk" / "punches the air"
  on the way down].

## Build plan

Everything below follows the repo's pipeline: clips authored as keyed
poses in `game/tools/blender/wrestling_clips.py` and rendered through
`clip_shot.tscn`; props as idempotent bpy scripts under `tools/blender/`
exporting deterministic `.glb`; closed on rendered frames.

**1. Per-wrestler entrance scripts** — `EntranceDirector` takes an
`EntranceScript` resource per roster entry instead of one shared timeline:
beats, walk speed, poses, lighting cues, pyro cues, camera shots and
lower-third in/out. Roman's and Cody's are the two above; Kenny keeps the
generic one.

**2. Clips** (`blender-animation` for keys and easing, `animation` for
blocking and frame counts):

- Cody: `Whoa_Arms` (arms flung wide, head back), `Air_Punch`,

only):

- Per-wrestler palettes: Roman gold/white on black; Cody full white with

- Capture-safe: cues fire from the entrance timeline only, never during a

**5. Camera** — the shot list above, as named `EntranceShot`s: the steadicam
