# Ambulance match

- **Win:** shut the other man in the back of the ambulance. No pinfalls, no
  submissions, no disqualification. The weapons are in play.
- **The ambulance:** `AmbulanceProp`, an original unbranded box-body ambulance
  built in code. It is white with a red band, a light bar, a stretcher and a
  bench inside, and two rear doors swung wide. It is backed down the entrance
  ramp after the entrances, its rear bumper at z = −7.4 (1.4 m up from the
  ramp foot), standing on the ramp's slope.
- **Getting him in:** the same `ShutIn` flow as the casket. He is whipped at
  the open back, goes up over the sill onto the floor of the box, and the
  doors are swung shut. Before the finish it is a near-miss: he shoves the
  doors back open and climbs out.
