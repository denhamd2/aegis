# AEW Fight Forever animation audit — Phase A (8 November 2026)

Phase A of [the remaining-animation plan](ff_remaining_plan_2026_11_08.md): audit
the 27 built moves before authoring anything new. Read-only desk audit on
Android/Termux; no Godot or Blender binary is present, so this is evidence from
the repository, the shipped `wrestling_clips.glb` and the recorded gates, not a
fresh engine run. Every "PASS" below means the desk-checkable criteria pass and
the Mac render/visual gate is still outstanding.

## Result

- **27/27 are built** (19 authored `ff_*` pairs + 8 reused moves), matching
  `FFMoveLibrary.progress()`.
- **24 pass every desk-checkable acceptance criterion.**
- **3 fail a measured criterion:**
  - `one_winged_angel`: impact at **1.333 s**, measured FF reference **0.9 s**
    (+0.43 s) and recovery at 2.20 s against 1.90 s.
  - `chokeslam_1`: built, but **unreachable in a configured match** — it exists
    only in `match.tscn`'s shared power pool, which every roster moveset replaces.
  - `squatting_samoan_drop`: hands **7 cm** from the hold against the 5 cm bar
    (a live `PairClearanceBaseline.GRIP` exception), plus 2 snaps in the
    defender clip.
- **0 dead holds and 0 snaps across all 38 authored FF clips.** Mean FF jerk is
  39.9 against the hero set's 44.2.
- **8 pass with a watch item**: five moves on jerk (six clips), two camera
  questions, and the springboard moonsault's snap/jerk. The clip-level pass does
  not settle the cross-cutting camera question in F4; if the review's wording is
  right, the cut has to move for all 17 close-cut moves.
- **No visual claim is made for any move.** Fresh per-move renders are still
  required, and no sheet is named for OWA.

## Method and evidence limits

- **Both roles authored**: `test_both_halves_of_every_paired_move_are_authored`
  requires an `authored` entry for both roles in `paired_recipes.gd` and both
  baked clips in `paired_poses.tres`. All 19 FF moves and all 5 paired reused
  moves qualify. The 3 reused special-system moves have both halves in the GLB
  (`Figure_Four`, `Springboard_Cutter`, `Springboard_Moonsault`) and are checked
  by their own tests.
- **Contact tick**: the authored impact frame in `wrestling_clips.py` /
  `paired_recipes.gd` vs `MoveDef.startup_frames / 60`. All 19 FF moves match
  within two ticks (one authored 30 fps frame) except OWA. For dives the contact
  is `DiveSpot`'s event (`SPRING_CONTACT` 48, `MOONSAULT_CONTACT` 66), not
  `startup_frames`; for the Figure-Four it is the 124-tick lock asserted by
  `test_own_hold`.
- **Grip and pair clearance**: the ratchet in
  `PairClearanceBaseline` and `test_pair_clearance.gd`. `OVER` is empty, so the
  last recorded run had every paired move inside the body/arm limits. `GRIP`
  has one live exception, `power_samoan_drop` (0.07 m). "≤5 cm" in the tables
  means no live exception; the test skips a hold that is in reach for fewer than
  10 frames.
- **Pose and landing**: `PoseLint.EXEMPT` is empty and
  `test_pose_lint.gd` covers every authored clip. `test_paired_poses.gd`
  asserts exact role/trajectory length parity, whole-body tracks and no
  face-plant; `test_paired_moveset.gd` keeps root keys on or above the mat and
  every paired animation reachable.
- **Cadence**: `tools/anim/ff_cadence_audit.py` samples the shipped GLB at
  30 fps with the same holds/snaps/jerk formula as
  `game/tests/test_clip_cadence.gd`. Calibration: the 20 hero clips total
  **883.6** against the committed **952.0** budget. The script is a
  bpy/Godot-free reproduction; the Godot gate remains authoritative.
- **Impact cut**: `MatchCamera._ff_grapple_cut` and `_is_moving_attack`, in the
  default Fight Forever coverage. FF grapples take the close cut from the start
  of the move and return wide at `startup/total − 0.03` progress
  (`FF_IMPACT_LEAD`). `running_*` files stay wide by design.
- **Not measured here**: fresh grip medians, actual 60 Hz frame playback, any
  render, M1 performance, or the Godot gate results. The baselines are the
  last recorded repository values.

## Findings

### F1 — OWA is 0.43 s slower than the measured reference (major)

`one_winged_angel` lands at 1.333 s (`80/60`) and ends at 2.20 s.
`gauntlet/refs/aew_ff_animation_review.md` measures the FF move landing at
**0.9 s** with Kenny up at **1.9 s**. This is the only per-move measured timing
failure. The clip and the recipe's `ff_one_winged_angel` trajectory must be
retimed together; the defender's key at frame 40 is the sit-out impact.

### F2 — `chokeslam_1` is built but unreachable in a configured match (major)

It is referenced only by `match.tscn` as the shared `power_move_pool`
(`ExtResource("ffc1")`). `TitleScreen.configure_match()` replaces each roster
entry's `power` tier, and none of Roman, Cody or Kenny lists `chokeslam_1`.
It is even in `MovePlaybook.OVERRIDES` as a mid-match move, so the omission is
easy to miss. The reachability test passes only because it inspects the
unconfigured scene. Decide whether to give it to Roman (a big man's chokeslam
fits), to another future roster entry, or to mark it library-only and stop
counting it as wired. Either way,
`test_the_match_scene_gives_both_wrestlers_the_whole_moveset` should configure
a match per roster entry so this cannot hide again.

### F3 — `squatting_samoan_drop` misses the 5 cm contact bar (major)

`PairClearanceBaseline.GRIP` still carries `power_samoan_drop: 0.07`, so the
last recorded median was above 5 cm. The defender clip also has 2 snaps.
Refit the contact, or fix the landing keys, before Phase B closes.

### F4 — The FF cut returns wide *before* contact (major, camera)

`_cut_ff_impact = startup/total − 0.03` and the mode ends when
`grapple_rig.progress() >= _cut_ff_impact` ("Out wide on the hit"). The impact
frame is therefore always in the wide shot. The measured review says the
**impact plays in the low close-up** and the camera cuts back wide for the
recovery. Either the review wording or the code is wrong; it affects every FF
move that takes the close cut. Settle it before judging impact emphasis.

### F5 — Running V-Triggers skip the close-up (minor, camera)

`_is_moving_attack` treats every `running_*` file as wide. The review records a
V-Trigger close-up of about 0.4 s around the knee. The standing V-Trigger
(`v_trigger_1`, Kenny's signature) gets a 0.26 s close; the running ones get
none. Verify which V-Trigger was measured and, if the running ones should cut,
stop classifying them with the blanket prefix.

Related: `_is_signature` also checks the file prefix, so FF signature-tier
moves (`code_breaker_1`, `v_trigger_1`, `powerbomb_v_trigger_combo`) get the
normal close, not the special lens and focus. Only OWA among the FF moves is a
finisher and takes the special treatment. Verify whether FF signatures should
use the special lens.

### F6 — `casualty_of_war` is misclassified for the camera (minor, camera)

It is a running lariat, but its file is `casualty_of_war.tres`, so
`_is_moving_attack` misses it and the close cut runs. The review says lariats
play out from the game camera. Classify by `animation_pair_id` or an explicit
set instead of the file prefix.

### F7 — The combo holds the close-up for 2.27 s (minor, camera)

The reference caps a close-up at about 1.7 s. `powerbomb_v_trigger_combo`
returns wide 0.09 s before the knee at 2.37 s, so the actual contact is wide
and the powerbomb half holds the close for 2.27 s. A combo likely needs a
second cut around the knee, not one shot from the start.

### F8 — The FF contact pull is off for the whole library (minor, known)

`PairedContacts.pulls()` returns false for every move under `moves/ff/`. The
recorded grips above were measured without it; enabling it dragged five holds
(the Stampede, fireman's carry bomb, Inverted DDT, OWA and Tiger Driver) up to
0.45 m off the fitted path. Keep it off until those five are refit with it.

### F9 — High-jerk clips to watch on frames (minor)

Mean FF jerk is 39.9; the hero mean is 44.2. These are above 60 and are the
first candidates for an in-between pass **if** the render shows a lurch:
`Code_Breaker_1_Attacker` 100.0, `Snap_Dragon_Suplex_2_Defender` 81.4,
`Snap_Dragon_Suplex_3_Defender` 73.6, `Buckle_Bomb_Defender` 63.4,
`Inverted_DDT_1_Attacker` 63.1, `Snap_Dragon_Suplex_3_Attacker` 61.7.
`Casualty_Of_War_Defender/Attacker` 57.7/56.3 and `Code_Breaker_1_Defender`
53.8 are just below. Some of these are intended acceleration (the Code Breaker
spring, a landing slap), so inspect the frames before re-keying.

### F10 — Oklahoma Stampede recovers in 0.5 s (minor, pacing)

The attacker is neutral at 1.90 s after an impact at 1.40 s, half the plan's
"about a second". It is a running powerslam that ends in a kneel and pops up;
check whether that reads rushed or deliberate when rendered.

### F11 — Visual evidence is incomplete (audit limitation)

Existing review sheets cover most batches:
`tiger_driver_98.png`, `batch1.png` (powerbomb, sit-out, spinebuster,
brainbuster, chokeslam), `ff_batch2_roman.png`, `ff_batch3_cody.png`,
`ff_batch4_buckle_bomb.png`, `ff_batch5_casualty_of_war.png`,
`ff_batch6_springboard_moonsault.png`, `ff_kenny_batch1_v_triggers.png`,
`ff_kenny_batch2_snap_dragons_combo.png`, `cross_rhodes_ff.png`,
`staples_chop_spear_setup.png`. No sheet is named for OWA — confirm whether it
is inside one of the Kenny batch sheets — and none of these are fresh per-move
six-beat renders. Do not close Phase A on the sheets alone.

2. **Resolve `chokeslam_1` ownership** and configure the reachability test (F2).
3. **Fix the Samoan Drop grip/landing** (F3).
4. **Settle the FF camera grammar** (F4) before touching individual cuts; then
   fix F5–F7 in the same camera pass.
5. **Render the six high-jerk clips** and add in-betweens only where frames
   justify it (F9).
6. **Check the Stampede recovery** in the same render pass (F10).

Nothing in Phase C (the remaining 119) should start until F1–F4 are settled.
The roster decision in the plan — add selected library moves to the existing
three, or build for a future roster — is still open and blocks Phase C.
