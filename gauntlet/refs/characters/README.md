# Character sheets

The owner's character sheets, kept here as the standing reference for each
character's model -- read these, not memory or an earlier render, before
changing a character.

| File | Character | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `aubrey_edwards_sheet.png` | Aubrey Edwards, the referee (1536 x 1024): face front, 3/4 and profile; full length front, sides, back and action; uniform details (shirt front, side, back, patch, belt, pack, shoes); expressions; hair; eye and make-up; ear and earring | `tools/blender/aubrey_aaa.py` |
| `kenny_omega_sheet.png` | Kenny Omega | the Kenny AAA rebuild (character_aaa_plan.md) |

Supplied by the owner, who has chosen to keep the real names, faces and
likenesses in the game. They are references, not game assets: nothing under
`game/` loads them.

Measuring off them. Scale a sheet by the character's height, feet to crown,
in its full-length front view (Aubrey: about 546 px for 1.756 m with hair and
shoes, so ~3.2 mm a pixel). Where the photo's posture differs from the rig's
(her collar and shoulders sit higher in the photo), place details by
landmarks -- fractions of the way between the collar points and the belt,
and of the chest's width out from the zip -- rather than by absolute height.
Note the Aubrey sheet's caption says the logo patch is on her left chest,
but its pictures show it on her right; the owner's call is the pictures.
