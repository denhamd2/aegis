**Verdict: REVISE the remaining source integration.**

boundaries should be corrected before relying on a groom to conceal them.

## Evidence

All three lit runtime views in `/tmp/aegis-kenny-judge/round7-surface2` and

## Remaining source faults

1. **Photo-to-scalp transition:** the forehead's lighter, textured photo patch
   still meets a much flatter scalp fallback in a soft rectangular transition.
   It is clearer in unlit, and remains visible in lit front/quarter views.
   Continuous polygon feathers do not alone establish a visually seamless
   colour/texture-frequency match. Integrate the two sides without returning
   to V1's horizontal bands or hiding the area beneath hair.
2. **Residual photo-hair and nape edge:** a distinct golden photo-hair tuft
   persists above the ear, and the rear scalp/nape ends in a sharp angled
   boundary. Separate intentional dark root support from these visible source
   image remnants; the future groom should not be the only thing masking them.

## Lit response is a separate issue

The lit lower face remains substantially darker than the unlit albedo read.
The coordinator's fixed normal/detail-off control has a reported facial-region
MAE of only 0.273/255, so the broad darkness cannot be attributed simply to
coarse pore/detail normals. Do not repeat that test or brighten the entire
albedo indiscriminately. Keep the smaller source-boundary corrections separate
from further assessment of the lit geometry/BRDF/lighting response against the
reference. No single cause for that response is established by this review.

After the two source integration corrections, a groom preview is the useful
next combined test. Keep the paired lit/unlit/clay evidence so improvements in
one cannot conceal failures in another. Engineering, effective-tint pixel
checks and mathematically smooth mask construction are supporting diagnostics;
they are not visual likeness scores. Full-arena appearance, animation, native
M1 frame pacing and a 10/10 outcome remain unverified.
