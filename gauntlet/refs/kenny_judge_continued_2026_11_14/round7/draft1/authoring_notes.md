The prior sixth result tied the published 4.6/10; it was recovery, not a supported visual win. The owner confirmed the four repository references and retained 10/10 as the acceptance aim. No regression is authorised for publication.

This stage starts from the packed **owned RAW head** and discards the legacy head fit/TABLE/RELAX stack. Explicit manual front/profile feature and visible-contour annotations are in `tools/blender/characters/kenny_reference_landmarks.json`, with approximate 3–5 px uncertainty and greater uncertainty at hair/beard-obscured edges. Front eye spacing fixes scale and roll; shared feature heights fix independent profile scale. Protected iris/pole geometry anchors registration. A single regularized polyharmonic warp fits brow/eye contour, nose bridge/tip/ala, lips, cheek/jaw/chin, with neck/back anchors and inverse-transpose normal transport. The same warp carries the mouth interior/teeth; the eyes remain exact.

These residuals are still substantial; the first draft is not accepted geometry. The held-out three-quarter camera is registered only from eyes/ear: nose error **24.7 px**, mouth **13.6 px**, chin **39.5 px**, exposing remaining shape/alignment uncertainty rather than proving likeness.
