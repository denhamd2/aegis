## Evidence inspected

The actual runtime `head_front`, `head_three_quarter` and `head_side` PNGs in

## Visible improvement

- The earlier forehead/brow wrinkles and lumpy cheek/jaw nodules are reduced.
- Surface transitions are less cluttered, making the head easier to evaluate
  without the previous texture and hair concealing or confusing its shape.
- This supports retaining the new reference-led workflow as a useful
  foundation. It does not by itself establish that the sculpt matches Kenny.

## Two highest-value corrections

1. **Forehead/brow and nasal projection.** Calibrate the held-out photograph's
   pose/pitch before sculpting to its residuals. In the current clay views the
   bridge/tip appear vertically tucked beneath the forehead, while the primary
   profile shows a more clearly projecting, relatively straight nasal profile.
   Fit the forehead/brow-to-bridge/tip relationship across front and profile,
   then verify the quarter view. More nostril width or darker eye shading would
   not address this relationship.
2. **Central chin and lower-face relationship.** Match chin shape/projection
   and mouth-to-chin vertical spacing after the same camera check. The current
   quarter-view lower face still feels gaunt/tucked, and the supporting overlay
   shows substantial chin displacement. The front jaw is already substantial:
   this is **not** a recommendation to widen the entire jaw. Use the front,
   profile and held-out contours together before making local volume changes.

The reported held-out nose/mouth/chin errors justify further inspection, but
camera/pose mismatch may account for part of them. They do not prove which
anatomical change is correct. Conversely, a low fitted-view residual would not
override a poor held-out visual match.

Preserve the established protected eyes, neck and rig while refining the
authoritative head. Brown irises remain the owner's preference and are not a
penalty. No hair, skin, deformation, full-arena or native performance claim can
be inferred from this clay-only review.
