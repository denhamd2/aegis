**Verdict: PASS — proceed to a surface preview only.**
This is permission to evaluate the clean head with coherent surface detail,
not completed anatomical/likeness acceptance and not a new overall score.

## Evidence

The actual runtime `head_front`, `head_three_quarter` and `head_side` images
in `/tmp/aegis-kenny-judge/round7-clay-draft2` were inspected against draft 1,
the round 6 clay controls and the confirmed primary

homology and reference-landmark overlay were inspected as supporting evidence.
The verdict follows visible shape, not the smaller fit residual, full fit
strength, Jacobian value, source fingerprints or rig checks.

## Why a surface preview is useful now

The forehead/brow and cheek/chin foundation is materially cleaner than the
old round 6 clay control. Its connected contours are coherent enough to
evaluate pigment and local detail without the previous nodular distortion
dominating every view. Draft 2 also avoids treating an incorrectly identified
upper-lid/canthus anchor as an iris centre. That correction improves confidence
in the comparison setup; it does not itself establish likeness.

The clay head does not yet independently demonstrate a strong Kenny identity.
The useful next test is a paired surface preview on this cleaner geometry,
rather than assuming that either a low numerical residual or an untextured
portrait alone settles the full likeness question.

## Remaining anatomical uncertainty

- The orbital hood and cheek-to-jaw transitions still read comparatively
  generic and planar. Preserve the paired clay views while evaluating the
  surface so pigment does not conceal an unresolved shape issue.
- The quarter-view mouth/central-chin relationship remains uncertain. Do not
  warp the chin to the reported 38.7px error until its anatomical correspondence
  is confirmed across front, profile and quarter views. The annotation may
  represent a different chin location rather than a correct sculpt target.
- Do not globally widen the jaw as a substitute for checking central chin
  projection and mouth-to-chin spacing. The front jaw already has substantial
  width.

## Conditions for interpreting the next preview

Keep the same views and show clay alongside the textured state. Surface detail
must not reintroduce the old floating eyebrow tracks or sculpted normal-map
lumps. Brown irises remain the owner's accepted preference. A subsequent
textured runtime review must judge actual face identity and material response;
this gate grants no head-score increase and no hair/material/global score.

The pass is deliberately limited to **proceeding to preview**. It is not a
publication verdict, an exact-reference match or proof of animation/native
performance quality.
