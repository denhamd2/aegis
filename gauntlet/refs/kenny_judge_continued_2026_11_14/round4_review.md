## Findings

- The coarse pale eyebrow fuzz is reduced. However, the upper face now reads
  as a heavy angular brow shelf. A visible outer-tail paint streak does not
  form a convincing continuous eyebrow. The exaggerated scowl and eye setback
  still differ from the references' more naturally hooded eye/brow structure.
- Broad straight-striped crown and rear-cap areas are exposed between sparse
  freestanding loops. The references have much denser overlapping rounded
  curl masses over these regions. Changing clump distribution has reduced
  resemblance despite technical changes in the atlas or renderer.
- The asymmetric forelock projects forward like a rigid handle in the
  three-quarter/side view. The target lock falls much closer to the forehead
  and temple. Some side/nape strands remain broad pale strips or stiff hooks.
- The body proportions remain broadly athletic and essentially unchanged.
  Existing coarse skin/beard treatment and shoulder/pectoral texture stretching
  continue to limit realism; the score does not require matching a particular
  photograph's wetness or red colour cast.

## Highest-value round 5 corrections

1. Use the planned grey control to fit brow/lid mass to the references and
   reduce the shelf, then place a subtle continuous eyebrow over the corrected
   contour. Preserve the protected eye geometry and owner iris preference.
2. Restore layered rounded curl clumps over the visible straight cap, following
   curved growth paths with dark roots and finer blonde transitions. More high
   loops alone will not solve the missing coverage or silhouette.
3. Bring the asymmetric forelock close to the forehead/temple and use finer,
   gravity-following curls rather than rigid freestanding hooks.

These are bounded next-round priorities, not a promised score increase. No
native performance, animation or full-arena likeness claim follows from these
static studio views. The user explicitly authorised continued rounds 4–6;
this review does not cancel or extend that scope.
