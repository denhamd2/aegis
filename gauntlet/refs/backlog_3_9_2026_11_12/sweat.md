# Sweat assessment

Isolated real Cody mesh, shipping material adapters, Godot 4.6.3 Forward+,
640×480, software Vulkan llvmpipe. Controlled lighting and camera are identical
across [early](cody_early.png), [middle](cody_middle.png) and [late](cody_late.png)
samples. See [manifest](cody_manifest.json) and `sweat_study` probe.

Wetness increases from .15 at the bell to .285 at 237.5 seconds / 150 damage,
then .42 at 475 seconds / 300 damage. Specialist review: chest/shoulder highlights
gain modestly; the face and broad body regions remain restrained. Retain the
existing tuning: there is no demonstrated benefit from increasing it further,
and the owner previously rejected oily skin.

This is a bind-pose material comparison, not a full-arena or native performance
measurement. Match-lighting acceptance remains with the owner on Mac.
