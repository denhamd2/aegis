## 1. Whip setup — 8 / 7 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 7 / 8 / 7 — 7.0, fail (borderline)

- **Works:** the rotational pull is there — beat order, whole-body grapple
  stance, foot placement and torso lean track the reference, and the
  defender stays balanced through the over-rotation sell. This is the
  hardest locomotion beat in the batch and it reads correctly.
- **Gap:** the grip is below resolution, so the clinch contact (two-handed
  wrist grip → release) cannot be verified; a contact close-up is required
  before sign-off.
- **Timing:** 1.20 s / 36f matches the reference window exactly.

## 2. Whip runner rebound — 8 / 7 / 4 / 6 / 7 / 6 / 8 / 7 — 6.5, fail

- **Works:** run/rebound lean and the low knee drop match the reference;
  the 0.40 s run at 9.0 m/s and the 0.50 s absorb with rope-load hold sit
  inside the reference's contact→push-off budget (0.46 s).
- **Gap:** no ropes in the solo render, so the chest-first contact, rope
  deflection and deepest-compression depth — the signature of this beat —
  are entirely unverified. Stride is slightly short at the far end.
- **Verdict:** fail. Re-render with ropes before re-review.

## 3. Whip return far stop — 8 / 7 / 4 / 6 / 7 / 7 / 8 / 6 — 6.5, fail

- **Works:** return run, deceleration and braking weight match the
  reference, and no interpenetration is visible; the pass-by is correctly a
  no-contact beat. 0.667 s at 7.0 m/s matches the reference cadence.
- **Gap:** the far-rope foot plant is below resolution, and the two-handed
  stop/hang pose at the ropes cannot be fully verified.
- **Verdict:** fail. One close-up of the braking contact unblocks most of
  this.

## 4. Rebound slam — 8 / 7 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 7 / 8 / 7 — 7.0, fail (borderline)

- **Works:** run/rebound/grab/lift/slam beats all match; the attacker stays
  standing and drops the defender from waist height exactly as the
  reference reads (spinebuster family, no attacker drop). Defender lands
  and stays down.
- **Gap:** impact contact fidelity is below resolution — this is the one
  move in the batch whose entire point is the impact, and the contact frame
  is unverified. The 1.633 s catch window sits inside the reference's
  1.85 s release→impact span, but timing alone cannot carry it.
- **Verdict:** fail until the impact pass renders a readable contact frame
  with mat compression.

## 5. Rebound hurricanrana — 9 / 7 / 4 / 7 / 8 / 7 / 8 / 7 — 7.5, pass (borderline)

- **Works:** the full six-beat sequence — setup, rebound, connection,
  inversion, flip, land — matches, and the defender sells and stays down;
  this is the best-verified down-sell in the batch. 1.333 s total with
  0.75 s contact is in the right band against the reference's 0.63 s
  take-off→impact.
- **Gap:** inversion head/leg alignment is below resolution; the signature
  scissor-lock pose must be verified at full resolution, plus the impact
  pass for the landing.
- **Verdict:** pass, borderline — contingent on the alignment close-up.

## 6. Running grapple inverted drop — 8 / 7 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 7 / 9 / 7 — 7.5, pass (borderline)

- **Works:** run/grapple/inversion beats match from 0.14, and the timing is
  the best in the batch: 2.20 s total with contact at tick 74 and impact at
  118 matches the reference to the tick (1.23 s / 1.97 s). Defender carried
  and sold, attacker's feet planted through the hip-extension lift as
  referenced.
- **Gap:** head/neck alignment at the inverted apex is below resolution —
  on a head-first drop this is a safety-readability issue, not just polish —
  and frame 0 is a probe lead-in artifact that must be trimmed.
- **Verdict:** pass, borderline — contingent on the apex close-up.

## Batch verdict

Two passes (both borderline), four fails. Structurally this is the
strongest batch yet: every move is identifiable and beat-accurate against
the reference, versus 2.5–3.0 for the first lift/slam pass. No 10s are
earned — every score is capped by the same two problems: the unbuilt
impact pass and signature contacts rendered below review resolution.

## Top three fixes

1. **Land the impact pass.** Build the shared contact template — a
   dedicated contact frame with mat compression and one recoil, a small
   white particle burst (reference has no slow-motion, no hit-stop), and at
   least two sell beats with an asymmetric settle. This lifts FX off the
   4/10 floor and unblocks selling on every move, and it is a hard
   prerequisite for the rebound slam.
2. **Full-resolution contact close-ups.** Grip for the whip setup,
   inversion head/leg alignment for the hurricanrana, head/neck apex for
   the inverted drop, impact fidelity for the slam, foot plant for the
   far-rope stop. These are the caps on every pose/realism score; none may
   stay below resolution at sign-off.
3. **Rebound environment pass.** Put ropes in the whip-runner solo render
   and verify chest-first contact, rope deflection and absorb depth; trim
   the running-grapple frame-0 probe artifact and the short far-end stride
   on the runner.
