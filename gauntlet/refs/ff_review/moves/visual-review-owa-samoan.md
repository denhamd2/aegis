# Visual review evidence — OWA and Samoan Drop (8 November 2026)

Reviewer: free visual-QA model (image input). Sheets are seven-beat side/close
views, defender tinted blue. Numeric scores withheld; this is the evidence for
the art-director pass.

## one_winged_angel

### Beat by beat

- **0.00** — close clinch; attacker's arms around the defender's upper body,
  defender's left arm around the attacker's shoulder/neck; feet planted.
- **0.20** — defender lifted roughly horizontal across the attacker's right
  shoulder/upper chest, above the attacker's head.
- **0.35** — defender higher and more fully extended horizontally above the
  shoulder line; attacker upright.
- **0.50** — peak lift; defender nearly horizontal above the head/shoulders,
  arms flailing upward; attacker straight.
- **0.65** — slam begins; attacker sits on the mat, defender's legs kick
  vertically while the torso comes down.
- **0.80** — impact happened off-frame; attacker seated, defender flat supine
  behind/beside him.
- **1.00** — attacker stands cleanly; defender remains flat.

### Visible defects

- **Grip/contact:** hands and grip points are too small to verify at this
  panel size. The defender appears supported at shoulder/thigh without visible
  hand pressure; strong suspicion of invisible/floating grip.
- **Foot planting:** attacker's feet stay in exactly the same grid position
  through the lift; no step, pivot or stance adjustment under a full-body
  load.
- **Weight/balance:** attacker stands perfectly upright and vertically stacked
  while holding a body horizontally overhead; no backward lean, knee flexion,
  heel rise or torso compression. The pose reads weightless.
- **Interpenetration:** at 0.50 the defender's horizontal body passes very
  close to or through the attacker's head/neck; no visible gap.
- **Pose readability:** 0.65 is the least readable beat; the flip mechanics
  and the guiding contact are muddled. Defender's arms flail with no purpose.
- **Silhouette:** at 0.35–0.50 the two bodies merge into one tall vertical
  stack; no negative space, heads overlap.
- **Impact/timing:** collision is entirely between 0.65 and 0.80; no impact
  frame, no compression, no recoil.
- **Landing/sell:** defender lands perfectly flat and static with no bounce,
  limb reaction, mat deformation or exhale; attacker stands immediately with
  no residual effort.
- **Floating:** defender reads as a rigid weightless plank during the carry,
  with no sag or compression at the contact points.

## squatting_samoan_drop

### Beat by beat

- **0.00** — close standing start; attacker's right arm around the defender's
  head/neck, defender's left arm around the attacker's waist/back.
- **0.20** — defender hoisted upward, body vertical/diagonal above the
  attacker's head.
- **0.35** — defender draped across the shoulders/upper back in a fireman's
  carry/rack.
- **0.50** — defender balanced on the shoulders, arched backward over the
  attacker's head, legs elevated; attacker upright.
- **0.65** — drop; attacker drops to his knees, torso driving forward, defender
  coming down in front.
- **0.80** — impact occurred between beats; attacker on knees, defender flat
  supine in front.
- **1.00** — attacker rises/kneels upright; defender remains flat.

### Visible defects

- **Grip/contact:** headlock contact at 0.00; exact grip is too small to
  verify, possible forearm/neck interpenetration. During the hoist and carry
  the hands are not clearly visible; risk of floating support.
- **Foot planting:** feet remain in the same spots through the hoist and
  shoulder carry; no stance adjustment, pivot or weight shift.
- **Weight/balance:** attacker bolt-upright under a full body draped over the
  shoulders and arched over his head; no lean, knee bend, spinal compression
  or heel pressure. Reads weightless. At 0.65 the drop reads as a state change
  rather than physical effort.
- **Interpenetration:** at 0.50 the defender's shoulders/upper back likely
  overlap the attacker's head/neck; no clearance visible.
- **Pose readability:** 0.35–0.50 merged mass; hard to tell where the defender
  ends and the attacker's shoulders begin. The guiding contact through the
  drop is unclear.
- **Silhouette:** poor negative space; the pair reads as one tall block.
- **Impact/timing:** collision is between 0.65 and 0.80; no impact frame,
  compression or bounce.
- **Landing/sell:** defender lands perfectly flat and static; attacker
  recovers immediately with no effort sell.
- **Floating:** defender reads as a rigid plank with no sag or compression
  during the carry.

## Summary

Both sheets share the same core pattern: invisible/weak grip during the lift,
no weight or load communication on the attacker, the impact frame is missing
between beats, and the landing is static with no sell. Grip details and small
interpenetrations are below reliable resolution at this panel size and need a
larger viewport before final sign-off.
