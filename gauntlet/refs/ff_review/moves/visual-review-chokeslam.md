# Visual review evidence — Chokeslam (8 November 2026)

Reviewer: free visual-QA model (image input). Seven-beat side/close sheet,
defender tinted blue. Numeric scores withheld; this is evidence for the
art-director pass.

## Beat by beat

- **0.00** — standing grapple, attacker's arms around the defender's upper
  body, defender's hands near his own chest/face.
- **0.20** — attacker closes in and appears to take the throat/upper chest
  with one hand, pulling the defender in; both feet grounded.
- **0.35** — defender lifted, feet off the mat, angled upward, held by the
  neck/upper-chest hand; attacker's feet planted.
- **0.50** — peak lift; defender fully elevated, body roughly vertical,
  suspended one-handed at/above head height; attacker upright.
- **0.65** — drop/impact; attacker bent forward, arms extending down, defender
  horizontal at floor level.
- **0.80** — post-impact; defender flat on his back, limbs straight; attacker
  bent slightly, recovering.
- **1.00** — aftermath; attacker more upright and stepping back; defender
  remains flat and still.

## Visible defects

- **Grip/throat contact:** at 0.20 the hand looks like it lands on the upper
  chest/collarbone rather than clearly closing on the throat; fingers are not
  readable, so a genuine grip cannot be confirmed. At 0.35–0.50 the defender
  is suspended by what reads as one hand at the neck/upper chest, with no neck
  indentation, thumb/finger placement or skin deformation visible; risk of an
  invisible constraint. Arms may interpenetrate torsos in the clinch.
- **Foot planting:** attacker's feet appear planted through 0.00–0.50 with no
  widened stance, heel dig or bracing for a one-handed lift. May shift/slide
  between 0.50 and 0.65; no replant before release.
- **Weight/balance:** attacker holds a full body one-handed at head height
  while fully upright, with no counter-lean, spinal flexion, leg bracing or
  centre-of-mass shift. Physically implausible, reads weightless. Defender is
  rigid and extended with no sag, limpness or resistance during the lift.
- **Interpenetration:** at 0.50 the supporting wrist/hand likely clips into the
  neck/shoulder. At 0.65 the defender's back/buttocks reach the floor with no
  mat compression; possible floor interpenetration. At 0.80–1.00 the body lies
  flush with the grid with no sink or imprint.
- **Lift/drop mechanics:** the lift goes from standing to fully elevated in two
  beats on a near-vertical path with no clear arc or pull-in phase; it happens
  too fast to read as a weighted lift. The drop goes from elevated vertical to
  horizontal at floor level in one beat, with no deceleration or impact frame.
  The actual slam impact is skipped between beats.
- **Pose readability:** panels are narrow; finger-level grip, face and exact
  contacts are not readable. The blue tint separates the roles.
- **Silhouette:** at 0.50 the peak lift reads as a chokeslam. At 0.65 the
  horizontal body reads as impact, but the moment of back-impact is not
  separable from post-impact rest.
- **Timing/impact:** no dedicated impact frame with deformation; at 0.65 the
  defender is already at floor level, at 0.80 already flat. No back-slap,
  exhale, mat compression or recoil.
- **Landing/sell:** at 0.80–1.00 the defender is perfectly flat, limbs straight
  and symmetrical, arms at his sides: zero sell, reads as a ragdoll. Attacker
  recovers quickly with no exertion or chest heave.
- **Floating/sliding:** defender floats on an unclear support during 0.35–0.50.
  Possible attacker foot slide at 0.50–0.65. At 0.65–0.80 the defender changes
  from horizontal-at-floor to flat-on-back with no roll or settle.

## Summary

The lift silhouette is readable, but the one-handed support is not credible,
the attacker shows no load, the impact frame is missing between 0.65 and 0.80,
and the landing is a static ragdoll with no sell. Grip and small
interpenetrations need a larger viewport to confirm.
