## Fixes

### 1. Hook Left — 4.0
1. Rebuild the lead-arm path: ~90° elbow at contact, forearm horizontal,
   fist/forearm across the jaw. Acceptance: 80–100° elbow flexion at tick 11,
   fist crosses the jaw's lateral plane, silhouette within 10° of reference.
2. Add lead-foot pivot and heel lift: 20–30° foot yaw, hip line ≥20° toward
   target, lead heel off the mat. Acceptance: contact−3 to contact+2.
3. Extend the follow-through over at least 5 ticks with monotonic
   deceleration; no guard-pop within 3 ticks of contact.

### 2. Front Kick Body — 6.0
1. Lean the torso back: hip ahead of the support foot, hip–shoulder vector
   ≥20° rearward. Acceptance: measured at tick 32.
2. Drive through contact, then snap-rechamber: foot reaches max penetration
   at contact+2 and re-chambers by contact+6.
3. Keep the kicking-side guard hand above shoulder through contact.

### 3. Turning Back Kick Body — 8.0 (pass)
1. Add a 2-tick contact hold.
2. Verify heel-first contact and rechamber by contact+4.
3. Use this pose as the template for the other heel strikes.

### 4. Clinch Knee Body — 6.0
1. Forward hip thrust: hips translate ≥10 cm past the support-foot line from
   contact−2 to contact; knee apex at tick 21.
2. Head pull: clinch hand moves ≥15 cm down/back from contact−4 to contact;
   elbow closes ≥20°.
3. Support heel lifts onto the ball, ≥2 cm at contact.

### 5. Running Flying Knee — 6.0
1. Add crash/impact absorption: centre of mass descends for ≥6 ticks after
   contact; the clip ends grounded or in a controlled fall. Standing recovery
   is a hard fail.
2. Keep the torso within 15° of horizontal at contact; retract the knee and
   arc down after contact.
3. Use a grounded recovery (roll-through or sprawl) matching the reference
   aftermath.

### 6. Side Kick KD — 8.0 (pass)
1. Verify knee extension ≥160° and heel contact; rechamber by contact+5.
2. Push through over 2 ticks; hips travel ≥8 cm from contact to contact+2.
3. Add a 2-tick contact hold.

### 7. Turning Back Kick KD — 7.5 (pass, borderline)
1. Re-verify heel-first contact and fold timing at full resolution.
2. Stay in stance at least 4 ticks after contact before the step-over.
3. Add a 2-tick contact hold.
