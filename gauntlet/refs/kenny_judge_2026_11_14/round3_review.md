## Evidence inspected

Direct comparison against the primary repository references:
`gauntlet/refs/characters/kenny_3view.jpg`, `kenny_omega_sheet.png`,
`kenny_hair_top.png` and `kenny_hair_back.png`. All four were inspected again
for this final review; previous generated review images were not the target.

All nine actual final runtime captures in `/tmp/aegis-kenny-judge/round3` were
inspected: `head_front`, `head_three_quarter`, `head_side`, `head_back`,
`hair_top`, `body_front`, `body_three_quarter`, `body_side` and `body_back`.
The fixed 768-square Forward+ studio views support bounded static comparison,
not exact photographic camera/lighting matching or native performance claims.

## What improved

- Revision 1's severe angular cheek shading regression remains substantially
  repaired. The final views do not show its cutout-like cheek patches.
- Revision 2's long, regularly spaced sideways flyaways are much reduced,
  giving a tidier and less comb-like silhouette.
- Crown/nape clumps now have more continuous shape than the earlier faceted
  zigzag mass. These are modest visual gains, not proof of natural hair.
- The athletic body remains broadly usable, with its chest-to-waist taper
  retained. Runtime ownership and source isolation earn no visual score.

## Remaining likeness limitations

1. **Head and face:** the face still reads as a generic, strongly scowling
   wrestler more than the supplied Kenny references. Brow/upper-lid and eye
   setback, nose profile and cheek-to-jaw/chin relationships remain approximate.
   Brown irises are the owner's retained preference and were not penalized.
2. **Hair:** the crown resembles rows of stiff swept-back loops, while the
   sides/back show broad pale ribbons and a comparatively straight curtain.
   The references have softer, irregular rounded curl masses, dark roots,
   asymmetric forelocks and finer transitions into blonde ends. The silhouette
   is cleaner than revision 2, but the hair still looks authored as strips
   rather than convincing curled fibres.
3. **Skin and materials:** coarse brow/skin detail, comparatively opaque beard
   coverage and residual stretched shoulder/pectoral texture patches limit
   realism. Hair highlight bands also read as solid pale strips. Reference
   wetness/redness varies; a single photo's orange/red colour cast was not a
   mandatory skin target.
4. **Body:** the broad athletic proportions are plausible, but specific torso,
   shoulder/forearm and neck transitions remain only an approximate match.
   Gear is secondary in this rubric and was not used to inflate likeness.

No new obvious static missing-geometry or opaque-sheet blocker was found in
these nine views. This supports publication as a bounded iteration for the
owner to assess on their Mac; it does **not** close Kenny likeness acceptance.
Animation, full-arena appearance, temporal hair behaviour and M1/16GB frame
pacing remain outside this static review. The reported 78 passing engineering
checks and cold build are separate evidence and do not alter these scores.
No fourth cosmetic loop is requested or implied by this review.
