## Rendered review

Godot 4.6.3, Forward+, Vulkan, llvmpipe/LLVM 19 software rasterisation,
960×540, fixed 60 fps clock, 12 settling frames. These are bounded bind-pose
studies using the real material adapters and set, with fog and glints disabled.
They omit the bowl, animated presentation, follow spot and camera fill.
They are **appearance studies, never Mac performance evidence**. Source
images are copied unchanged; adjacent JSON records scope and image hashes.

| Study | Baseline materials | Revised materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roman | [Image](review/roman_before.png), [metadata](review/roman_before.json) | [Image](review/roman_after.png), [metadata](review/roman_after.json) |
| Cody / coat | [Image](review/cody_before.png), [metadata](review/cody_before.json) | [Image](review/cody_after.png), [metadata](review/cody_after.json) |

Both sides use the same bind pose, lens and neutral key tint. Roman's camera
is `(0, 1.68, -24.2)`, Cody's `(-0.7, 1.68, -24.2)`, aimed at
`(0, 1.44, -26)`, FOV 34. Key position/energy and skin settings change as
recorded in the metadata; these comparisons evaluate that combined pass.

Observed: wet highlights now appear on skin and directional shading has more
contrast. This does not supply the reference's facial anatomy, natural hair,
fine skin relief or coat construction. Hair remains shell-like, Roman's beard
reads as a solid mass, and Cody's coat needs material/geometry refinement.
The raw coat study does not validate its animated fit or handoff.

The bounded wide-stage attempts show severe white halos on fixture lenses
on both baseline and revised materials, even with fog/glints disabled.
Those images were rejected for judging truss/exposure quality. The extra
reflection probe separately causes black output; hiding it restores the
character and stage. SSR/SSIL/SSAO/glow ablation did not make it acceptable.
The probe therefore remains disabled in normal play.
