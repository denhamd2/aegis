## In 2K26's signature and finisher lists but not built

- **Roman:**
  - Superman Punch to a cornered or grounded man (ours is the running version);
  - Superman Punch into the Spear (the "super");
  - rapid-fire corner clotheslines, his real-life staple.
- **Cody:** the springboard Cody Cutter (ours is the standing and off-the-ropes version).

## Recommendation, in order of what the owner will notice

1. **Cross Rhodes from behind.** It is the 2K26 finish four times over. This is a new paired move, with the cinematic finisher camera reused.
2. **Cody's top-rope dive** (a moonsault onto a downed man).
   - The corner climb (`corner_climb`) already exists.
   - The new parts: a paired landing on the downed man, and the AI walking Cody to the near corner.
3. **Roman's corner clotheslines.** The corner-trapped state and flurry exist; this swaps Roman's corner punches for running clotheslines.
4. **Possum attack.** A downed man's leg sweep when the other stands over him, on a reversal press.
5. **Superman Punch → Spear** and the springboard Cutter.
