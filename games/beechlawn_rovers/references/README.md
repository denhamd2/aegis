# Beechlawn reference archive

This is the durable, project-local archive for the reference material reviewed for Beechlawn Rovers. Keep it in this project; do not mix these files into Aegis wrestling assets. The source images are preserved unedited. `SHA256SUMS` records file checksums.

## Stadium supporters, docks and tifo — 25 October 2026

`stadium_supporters_2026_10_25/` archives the owner gameplay/goal references and
public stadium/dock study photographs, with URLs and SHA256 in `provenance.json`.
`tifo_2026_10_25/` preserves the five later uploads: Everton display scale and
the supplied man's likeness. Study photos stay out of the game package; the
tifo is a generated painted silhouette print, white outline and quoted wording.
The crowd source is pinned MIT Microsoft Rocketbox, not a paid/extracted game
asset. See the production brief and shipped supporter license for source scope.

## UCL 26/27 ball — approved 25 October 2026

`source_uploads/chat_attachments/ucl_26_27_ball/league_size5/` preserves the four primary original screenshots: 70119 black UCL logo/surface macro; 70118 LGE replica/branding angle; 70117 opposite star pattern; 70116 adidas/FIFA Quality/front. The owner explicitly selected this **League size-5 replica**, not the Pro ball. Match the black logos and visible 1017531 marking. `mini_supplementary/` preserves the earlier five mini screenshots; use only for supplementary pattern detail, never pink logos or MINI labelling. `product_views/` has three higher-resolution Pro:Direct product views of KE9765; URLs/hashes and authority are in `provenance.json`. Reference-only research views stay out of the Mac export. Original/reconstructed artwork is for the owner's personal game; no trademark or asset licence is implied.

The default ball is authored in `tools/blender/beechlawn/ucl_26_27_league.blend`; its repeatable builder and native proof are described in `docs/art_briefs/ucl_ball_2026_10_25/brief.md`. Preserve the supplied worn-ball source and shipped model as the alternate option.

- OpenStreetMap supplement: the owner's screenshot is archived at `source_uploads/chat_attachments/openstreetmap/69078.jpg`. It shows the target western field immediately southwest of the Kilmore Drive / Beechlawn Close bend, with the neighboring green field to its east. Use it to anchor the field within the park and orient the residential/road boundary. The screenshot is a cropped mobile view without a scale, coordinates, or measurement overlay; use the gameplay specification's 105 m × 68 m field dimensions until the OSM feature geometry or a surveyed measurement confirms a different size. Do not infer exact dimensions from screenshot pixels.
- Google Earth supplement: the owner's `69084.jpg` is archived at `source_uploads/chat_attachments/google_earth/69084.jpg`. It shows the surrounding St. John Vianney Football Club grounds from above, coordinate grid labels around the Kilmore Drive area, and a 100 m scale bar. This adds scale and satellite detail for nearby pitch orientation, grass, tree belts, hard boundaries, and residential edges. It appears to show the more formal neighboring grounds east of the target western park field; use it to understand the adjacent complex and broad site scale, not as a replacement for the target-field placement from OSM. The UI and coordinate grid obscure some linework, so don't claim surveyed target-pitch dimensions from this single screenshot.

## Road-facing placement constraint

The original uploaded satellite view and `google_captures/satellite/` show
substantial park grass between the target pitch and Kilmore Drive's pavement.
Use that band when placing the roadway, houses, cars and services; the street
must not sit directly beside the touchline. The current 35 m grass / 37.75 m
road-edge settings are authored approximations, not measurements of the site.
See `../docs/art_briefs/kilmore_road_setback.md` for the comparison and limits.
