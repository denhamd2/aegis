# Beechlawn reference archive

This is the durable, project-local archive for the reference material reviewed for Beechlawn Rovers. Keep it in this project; do not mix these files into Aegis wrestling assets. The source images are preserved unedited. `SHA256SUMS` records file checksums.

## Contents

- `source_uploads/drive_folder/` — all 27 files downloaded from the owner's shared Drive folder, retaining their original downloaded filenames: 26 PNG reference boards and the satellite-map screenshot.
- `source_uploads/source_assets/` — original owner-supplied Y-Bot and worn-ball ZIP packages, retained alongside the visual references because they are the source inputs for the footballer rig and ball asset.
- `source_uploads/chat_attachments/character_roster_sheets/` — the five directly uploaded character sheets from the roster upload.
- `source_uploads/chat_attachments/title_player_photos/` — original photos supplied for the title-screen revisions: `1-69003.jpg` (David Murray), `2-69005.jpg` (Robbie Roden, prior art iteration), `3-69001.jpg` (Derek Mitchell, former art iteration), and `4-title-player-selfie.jpg` (owner likeness used for Derek Mitchell, #3). Use David and the owner selfie for current promotional art; Uhlsport is the confirmed kit manufacturer.
- `source_uploads/chat_attachments/club_crest/69032.jpg` — authoritative later club logo upload: tall navy/white oval, leafless green tree, EST 2004 and green/white lower stripes. Preserve its approximately 0.71 width/height ratio rather than the round generated chest badges.
- `source_uploads/chat_attachments/openstreetmap/` — the owner-uploaded OpenStreetMap screenshot `69078.jpg`, showing the park blocks, adjacent streets, and mapped green field footprints near Kilmore Drive.
- `source_uploads/chat_attachments/google_earth/` — the owner-uploaded Google Earth screenshot `69084.jpg`, showing satellite imagery, coordinate grid, and a 100 m scale bar over the nearby St. John Vianney Football Club grounds.
- `source_uploads/chat_attachments/football_reference_upload_round_1/` and `...round_2/` — both direct uploads of the five football/gameplay reference JPGs. These uploads are byte-identical duplicates where they share a checksum; both occurrences are retained to preserve provenance.
- `google_captures/street_view/` — the panorama from the shared [Kilmore Drive Street View](https://maps.app.goo.gl/p7TfFiB6Qmqviwsh6), eight headings at 45-degree intervals, the link's original heading, and a contact sheet.
- `google_captures/satellite/` — browser captures of the shared [Kilmore Drive satellite view](https://maps.app.goo.gl/SPqee19XAk4EQ6qs7): estate overview, pitch view at zoom 19, and close pitch view at zoom 20.

- Original Google links: [Street View](https://maps.app.goo.gl/p7TfFiB6Qmqviwsh6) and [satellite view](https://maps.app.goo.gl/SPqee19XAk4EQ6qs7).
- OpenStreetMap supplement: the owner's screenshot is archived at `source_uploads/chat_attachments/openstreetmap/69078.jpg`. It shows the target western field immediately southwest of the Kilmore Drive / Beechlawn Close bend, with the neighboring green field to its east. Use it to anchor the field within the park and orient the residential/road boundary. The screenshot is a cropped mobile view without a scale, coordinates, or measurement overlay; use the gameplay specification's 105 m × 68 m field dimensions until the OSM feature geometry or a surveyed measurement confirms a different size. Do not infer exact dimensions from screenshot pixels.
- Google Earth supplement: the owner's `69084.jpg` is archived at `source_uploads/chat_attachments/google_earth/69084.jpg`. It shows the surrounding St. John Vianney Football Club grounds from above, coordinate grid labels around the Kilmore Drive area, and a 100 m scale bar. This adds scale and satellite detail for nearby pitch orientation, grass, tree belts, hard boundaries, and residential edges. It appears to show the more formal neighboring grounds east of the target western park field; use it to understand the adjacent complex and broad site scale, not as a replacement for the target-field placement from OSM. The UI and coordinate grid obscure some linework, so don't claim surveyed target-pitch dimensions from this single screenshot.

## Road-facing placement constraint

The original uploaded satellite view and `google_captures/satellite/` show
substantial park grass between the target pitch and Kilmore Drive's pavement.
Use that band when placing the roadway, houses, cars and services; the street
must not sit directly beside the touchline. The current 35 m grass / 37.75 m
road-edge settings are authored approximations, not measurements of the site.
See `../docs/art_briefs/kilmore_road_setback.md` for the comparison and limits.

## Traced park footprint

`research/kilmore_site/site_trace.json` preserves the manual boundary pixel picks,
image hash and transformation into the authored 105 m pitch coordinate system.
It informs the tapered western green and continuing roadside. It is an
approximate modelling alignment, not a survey. See
`../docs/art_briefs/kilmore_site_footprint.md`; original imagery stays unedited
and is excluded from game exports.
