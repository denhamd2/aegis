# Home-end supporter tifo

Owner references: `references/tifo_2026_10_25/`, photos 70198/70197/70196
for scale and deployment; 70201/70199 for the same man's likeness. Generated
painted supporter illustration; normal healthy eyes, no black eye. The owner
requested a silhouette cutout with a white shaped outline and transparent
background, and the exact quoted text:

> "I KNOW CATHY CAN BE A BITCH SOMETIMES"

The only navy panel is the connected quote section below the portrait. No
rectangular background, laurels or outer frame remains. Original generated
outputs are preserved outside the repo under `/workspace/generated_images`.
Runtime uses the RGBA print with alpha scissor, not blended transparency.

`build_tifo.py` authors a 22 × 28 m grid (10,240 triangles), UVs, editable
Blender source and GLB. The shader unfolds a gathered top concertina into its
full length and adds restrained fabric motion. Fixed upper attachments and
finite GPU deformation avoid a high-resolution CPU cloth simulation at match
time. This is directed cloth presentation, not a physical textile solver.

The venue presentation profile owns mounting, print and three camera stations.
The wide angle reveals it over six seconds; close and oblique angles show the
full image and quotation. It is cleared on intro completion or skip, leaving
the live match unobstructed. Kilmore does not instantiate a stadium tifo.
