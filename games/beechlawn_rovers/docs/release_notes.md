# Beechlawn Rovers 27 — v0.3.0

Assisted player switching follows real passes and selects appropriate defenders,
with a stable Tab override and automatic goalkeepers. Options can retain manual
switching. A compact black-and-white match HUD adds player identity/stamina,
shot charge, a clearer radar and F1 keyboard help; blue intro graphics stay intact.

Select Kilmore Drive or the new large Rovers Arena in Options. The original
Blender stadium has blue seating, crowd/seat LOD, maintained striped turf,
regulation markings and corner flags. Both venues use the existing live goals,
animated nets and football rules. Kilmore keeps its uneven ground and puddles.

This package also includes the pending shared possession skills, calibrated
foot-plant locomotion, high/full-stretch goalkeeper saves and Shane/John head
refinements. No paid or extracted PES/FIFA assets are included. Mac-only.

Cloud logic/package checks and native Forward+ captures do not establish native
Mac performance or final character/stadium likeness acceptance.

# Beechlawn Rovers 27 — v0.2.3

Player Des Feeney now has short dark hair around the sides and back with a
bare crown, based on the newly archived original photos. Both Des identities
have rounder eyelid openings and corrected fitted blinks. All eleven Boardeaux
slots reuse the player version; the referee keeps his separate head and groom.

Shared body and garment fullness gives John and player Des fuller builds while
retaining the common rig and animations. Updated individual anatomy, skin,
hair and close-up geometry reuse shared texture resources. Per-player
materials and morph weights survive distance-detail changes.

Fixed-tick bone poses remain authoritative for ball contact and recorded
replays, with interpolation for rendering. Cinematic-only depth of field and
restrained motion-vector blur restore cleanly on cuts, skips and return to
gameplay. Options saves these settings and optional TAA; baseline MSAA stays
the default.

Short curved near turf and intermediate detail are available across the pitch
through projected-size selection and bounded spatial chunks. Mud, puddles and
paint retain their existing masks; default visible detail is capped at
205,568 triangles within a 240k budget.

Validation includes native Forward+ software captures, source/export and
shared-resource checks, contact/replay regressions and a complete unskipped
three-minute 11v11 match. The Mac package records its version, source commit
and packaging UTC on the title screen.

Individual facial likeness and collar/shoulder fitting remain in progress.
Native M1 Pro appearance, moving visual polish, balanced live goalkeeper
outcomes and 1080p60 performance remain open acceptance gates.

Mac: extract Beechlawn-Rovers-Mac.zip and open the app. The package contains
universal Apple Silicon/Intel binaries and is ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
